# QubWatch

## AI-Powered Business Monitoring and Investigation Platform

QubWatch is a business monitoring and investigation platform designed to help business owners detect unusual transactions, identify potential losses, monitor operational activities, and investigate suspicious patterns.

The platform is designed to give business owners greater visibility and control over what happens in their business, especially when they are away from the business or unable to personally supervise daily operations.

## 1. The Problem

Many business owners rely heavily on employees to manage sales, inventory, cash, products, and daily transactions.

When the owner is absent, sick, travelling, or otherwise unable to supervise the business, losses may occur through:

* Unrecorded sales
* Unauthorized discounts
* Suspicious refunds or reversals
* Inventory discrepancies
* Unusual transaction patterns
* Manipulation of business records
* Unauthorized activities by staff
* Other operational irregularities

Traditional business records may show what happened without helping the owner understand what deserves attention.

QubWatch aims to help solve this problem by continuously analysing business activity and bringing unusual patterns to the owner's attention.

## 2. The Goal

The goal of QubWatch is to provide business owners with a simple system that helps them:

1. Monitor business activities.
2. Detect unusual or suspicious patterns.
3. Receive alerts about activities requiring attention.
4. Investigate suspicious transactions and events.
5. Understand possible causes of losses.
6. Use AI to analyse available business information.
7. Make better-informed business decisions.

## 3. Target Users

The primary users of QubWatch are:

* Small business owners
* Medium-sized business owners
* Entrepreneurs
* Business managers
* Operations managers
* Finance and accounting personnel
* Authorized investigators or administrators

The initial version will focus primarily on business owners and authorized business managers.

## 4. Core Product Areas

QubWatch will initially contain the following major areas:

### Login

Allows authorized users to securely access their QubWatch account.

### Business Setup

Allows a business owner to create and configure their business profile, including basic business information, products, and users.

### Dashboard

Provides an overview of important business activities and potential issues.

The dashboard may display:

* Total transactions
* Revenue
* Products
* Inventory information
* Alerts
* Suspicious activities
* Investigations
* Recent activities

### Products

Allows authorized users to create and manage products or services being monitored.

Product information may include:

* Product name
* Category
* Price
* Quantity
* Stock level
* Status

### Transactions

Records business transactions that can be analysed by QubWatch.

Transaction information may include:

* Date and time
* Product or service
* Quantity
* Amount
* Staff/user responsible
* Transaction type
* Payment method
* Transaction status

### Alerts

Identifies activities that may require attention.

Examples include:

* Unusual transaction amounts
* Repeated refunds
* Unusual discounts
* Abnormal transaction frequency
* Unexpected inventory changes
* Other unusual patterns

Alerts should provide enough information for the user to understand why the activity was flagged.

### Investigation

Allows the business owner or authorized user to examine an alert or suspicious activity.

An investigation may include:

* Alert details
* Related transactions
* Date and time
* Staff/user involved
* Relevant business records
* Investigation notes
* Evidence
* Investigation status
* Findings

### AI Assistant

Provides an AI-powered interface through which authorized users can ask questions about their business data.

Examples:

* "Why was this transaction flagged?"
* "Show me unusual transactions from this week."
* "Which products have unusual activity?"
* "What changed compared with last week?"
* "Help me understand this alert."

The AI assistant should use authorized QubWatch business information when generating responses.

## 5. Initial User Flow

The initial QubWatch user journey will be:

Login
→ Business Setup
→ Dashboard
→ Products
→ Transactions
→ Alerts
→ Investigation
→ AI Assistant

## 6. Core Principle

QubWatch is intended to assist business owners with **visibility, detection, and investigation**.

QubWatch should not automatically accuse an employee of theft or wrongdoing simply because an activity appears unusual.

An alert indicates that an activity may require review.

Final decisions should remain with the authorized business user.

## 7. Initial MVP

The first Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will focus on:

* User login
* Business setup
* Product management
* Transaction recording
* Basic dashboard
* Rule-based alerts
* Alert details
* Basic investigation workflow
* Initial AI assistant

More advanced analytics, integrations, automated detection models, reporting, and additional business features can be introduced in later versions.

## 8. Future Direction

Future versions of QubWatch may include:

* Advanced anomaly detection
* Inventory monitoring
* Automated reports
* Staff activity analytics
* Financial integrations
* Point-of-sale integrations
* Notifications
* Mobile application
* Role-based access control
* Audit trails
* Advanced AI investigation
* Business performance analytics

## 9. Project Vision

QubWatch aims to become an intelligent business monitoring system that helps business owners see what is happening in their businesses, identify what deserves attention, and investigate unusual activity before small problems become major losses.
